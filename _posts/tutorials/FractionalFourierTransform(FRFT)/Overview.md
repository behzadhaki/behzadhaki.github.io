In this tutorial, we will start with a quick refresher on the Fourier Transform (FT).

Subsequently, we introduce the concept of the Fractional Fourier Transform (FRFT) and discuss its properties and how it relates to the FT.

Then, we will explore how to utilize the FRFT for sound synthesis and processing.
