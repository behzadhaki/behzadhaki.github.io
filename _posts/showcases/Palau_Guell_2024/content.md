

## Overview

In November 2024, Raül Refree gave an improvised performance on the MIDI-enabled pipe organs of Palau Güell, Antoni Gaudí's landmark building in Barcelona, as part of the *Organic* concert series.

Refree played the venue's main organ from the third floor, out of sight of the audience, while two portable organs played back accompaniments generated in real time. To turn our rhythm-only system into one that could produce harmonic material, we paired the GrooveTransformer with a Markov-based Max device trained live on Refree's playing. The GrooveTransformer generated the accompaniment rhythms, and each onset triggered the Markov models to choose the next pitch and note duration. No pre-composed material was used, so the accompaniment reflected only what was improvised in the moment. Nicholas Evans supervised the system during the performance, shaping its density, transposition and note durations.
